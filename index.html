Ruru privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Ruru is a personal email sorter. It runs on one Mac owned by its developer, Alan Molina, and sorts only his own Gmail accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What Ruru reads from Gmail

Ruru asks Google only for the gmail.modify permission, which lets it read messages and change their labels. Ruru reads new messages in the Inbox: sender, subject, body, headers and attachment names, and the text of some attachments. It uses this to decide whether a message is marketing, an update, a receipt, personal, sensitive or suspicious.

What Ruru changes in Gmail

Ruru adds its own labels (for example "Ruru/Marketing") and removes the Inbox label from messages you approve, or from categories you switch on. Every move is logged and can be undone. Ruru never sends, deletes or forwards email.

Where the data goes

  • Messages are classified by a language model running on the same Mac (Ollama). Message content is not sent to any outside AI service for sorting.
  • Copies of messages and Ruru's decisions are kept in a local database on that Mac.
  • Daily backups of that database are encrypted (AES-256) before they are written to the Mac and to the owner's iCloud Drive. Only the owner holds the key.
  • During occasional tuning sessions, the owner may review message headers (such as sender and subject) with Anthropic's Claude to improve Ruru's rules. Each such review is started and approved by the owner. These sessions will end once sorting is accurate.
  • The Gmail sign-in token is stored in the macOS Keychain. Ruru never sees your Google password.

Ruru does not sell, share or use Gmail data for advertising, and no person other than the owner reads it.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Ruru's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove Ruru's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The local data can be deleted by removing Ruru's data folder.
